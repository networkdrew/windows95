The Lineup

Michael Jordan Himself. The Chicago Bulls legend gets pulled off the golf course by a gang of cartoon rabbits and has to save Bugs, Daffy, and the rest of the Looney Tunes gang from being enslaved on an alien theme-park planet.
Bugs Bunny Voiced by Billy West. Recruits Michael for the biggest game of his career and mostly just wants to know "what's up, Doc?"
Daffy Duck Voiced by Dee Bradley Baker. Team captain in spirit if not in skill.
Lola Bunny Voiced by Kath Soucie. New to the Looney Tunes roster for this film, and the only one who can actually hoop.
Porky Pig / Tweety Voiced by Bob Bergen, holding down two spots on the bench.
The Tasmanian Devil Also voiced by Dee Bradley Baker. Not exactly known for his ball-handling.
Swackhammer Voiced by Danny DeVito. The owner of Moron Mountain amusement park, and the reason any of this is happening.
Stan Podolak Played by Wayne Knight. Michael's exasperated publicist.
Juanita Jordan Played by Theresa Randle.
Himself (cameo) Bill Murray shows up on the course in the film's climax, because of course he does.
