Behind the Jam

It all started with a shoe commercial. The "Hare Jordan" Nike ads that paired Michael Jordan with Bugs Bunny on the court were popular enough that Warner Bros. built an entire feature around the idea.
Directed by Joe Pytka and produced by Ivan Reitman's company, the film mixes live-action basketball with traditionally animated Looney Tunes in the same frame -- a blend that took a specialized animation crew to pull off convincingly for a full-length movie.
Lola Bunny was created specifically for this film -- she'd never appeared in a Looney Tunes short before Space Jam.
The film opened in U.S. theaters on November 15, 1996, and went on to become (at the time) the highest-grossing basketball movie ever made.
The soundtrack was a hit in its own right, headlined by R. Kelly's "I Believe I Can Fly," which won a Grammy for Best Male R&B Vocal Performance.
